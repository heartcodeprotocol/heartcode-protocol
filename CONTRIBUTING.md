# Contributing to Heartcode Protocol

Heartcode Protocol is an experimental public draft. Contributions should strengthen its clarity, testability, evidence base, and resistance to evaluator bias.

## Useful Contributions

- test cases that expose ambiguity or failure;
- critiques of a principle or definition;
- proposed scoring improvements;
- examples of reasonable evaluator disagreement;
- relevant empirical research;
- corrections to unsupported or unclear claims;
- proposals for new principles or narrower definitions.

## Contribution Standard

A proposed change should explain:

1. the problem;
2. the evidence or example demonstrating it;
3. the exact change proposed;
4. the expected benefit;
5. foreseeable tradeoffs or objections.

## Corrections Are Data

Do not erase the history of a correction. Significant changes should preserve:

- the earlier wording;
- the reason it was challenged;
- the evidence considered;
- the final decision;
- unresolved disagreement.

## Test Cases

Use [`evaluation/test-case-template.md`](evaluation/test-case-template.md) whenever practical.

Remove or anonymize personal information unless publication is authorized and necessary for evaluation.

## Conduct

Critique ideas, evidence, definitions, and methods. Do not attack contributors or speculate about their motives.
